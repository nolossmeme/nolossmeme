# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in NoLossMeme to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers to NEAR and Rust.
- Disagree about code, not people. Give and take feedback gracefully.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Shilling, scams or price manipulation in project spaces. NoLossMeme is a no-loss experiment, not financial advice.

## Enforcement

Maintainers (see `.github/CODEOWNERS`) may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently. Report problems privately to a maintainer through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/nolossmeme/nolossmeme/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct"; reports are kept confidential.
